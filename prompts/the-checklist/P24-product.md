# P24 · Product

Checklist: [the-checklist.md § Product](../the-checklist.md)

## What this step decides

The product-side go/no-go: critical journey, empty states, understandable errors, mobile, accessibility critical path, analytics, onboarding.

## How to think about it

- This is verification, status VERIFIED, not PLANNED. Run it on the production build and watch it work.
- Empty states and errors are where first-time users meet the product. Check them on purpose.
- Reuse the P17 · End-to-end journeys as the evidence.
- Accessibility here means the critical path only, set in P4.

## Options and trade-offs

No real fork here. Do the items, and record any `[N/A]` or `[DEFER]` with its reason or trigger.

## How to choose: solo founder / pre-revenue defaults

- Walk the core journey yourself on a phone and a desktop, fresh account.
- Fix launch blockers only; log the rest to the backlog.

## Human in the loop: escalate when

- A defect in the core journey that would need a scope or deadline change.
- Accessibility failure on a legal requirement from P1.

## Done means

- Each item verified with notes on what was run, where and when.
- Failures are fixed or recorded as accepted by Aviv.

## Depends on / feeds into

Phase depends on all required prior gates. Launch checks verify earlier work; they do not replace it. Fed by P4 · UX, UI, accessibility and i18n, P17 · End-to-end, P20 · Analytics and experimentation.
