# P26 · Growth

Checklist: [the-checklist.md § Growth](../the-checklist.md)

## What this step decides

How you get and keep users after the product works: landing page, positioning, SEO, social, email capture, referral, share, community, channels, content, feedback loops, funnels, churn.

## How to think about it

- Only after the product is operational. Growth before retention is a leaky bucket.
- Talk to users first. The customer interview loop and feedback collection tell you what to build and say.
- Pick one or two channels and give them time, rather than doing all of them badly.
- Measure with the P20 funnels and retention events. Experiments need enough traffic (P20).
- Any email capture, referral or tracking has privacy and consent duties (P1, P7).

## Options and trade-offs

| Channel type | Good for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Own content, SEO, community | Compounding, low cash | Slow, time heavy |
| Paid ads | Fast tests | Cash, needs conversion data |
| Referral and sharing loops | Products with natural sharing | Build effort, abuse risk |

## How to choose: solo founder / pre-revenue defaults

- Start free: landing page, interviews, one channel, email capture.
- Defer paid acquisition until activation and retention are known and positive.
- Review growth numbers weekly in P25 · Weekly.

## Human in the loop: escalate when

- Any paid acquisition spend or new paid growth vendor.
- Referral rewards that move money or credits.
- Tracking, email or data-collection changes that need consent or change compliance scope.
- Public statements making claims about the product.

## Done means

- Chosen channels have a metric and a review date.
- Funnel and churn are measured with real data.
- Items not pursued are `[DEFER]` with a trigger.

## Depends on / feeds into

Depends on the product being operational (P24, P25). Fed by P20 · Analytics and experimentation and P21 · SEO / discoverability. Feeds P25 · Weekly and P20 (experiment pipeline).
