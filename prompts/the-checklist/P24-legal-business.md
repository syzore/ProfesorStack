# P24 · Legal/business

Checklist: [the-checklist.md § Legal/business](../the-checklist.md)

## What this step decides

Whether the business side is ready: legal documents, disclosures, pricing, taxes, payment flow, refund/cancellation, support contact, domain/email.

## How to think about it

- Which documents and disclosures are required comes from P1. Do not invent legal specifics here.
- Payment, pricing, tax and refund behavior must match what you state publicly.
- Every paid capability needs billing failure behavior (checklist rule): failed card, double charge, dispute.

## Options and trade-offs

No real fork here. Do the items, and record any `[N/A]` or `[DEFER]` with its reason or trigger.

## How to choose: solo founder / pre-revenue defaults

- Confirm required legal text with counsel or Aviv before publishing. Use the P1 compliance deliverable as the list.
- Test payments in test mode, then one live low-value transaction that Aviv approves.

## Human in the loop: escalate when

- Publishing any legal document or disclosure.
- Setting prices or tax handling; moving payments to live mode; any money moved.
- Compliance scope change.
- Refund or cancellation terms that differ from law or the stated policy.

## Done means

- Documents published and match behavior.
- Payment, refund and cancellation flows verified.
- Support contact and email are tested.

## Depends on / feeds into

Phase depends on all required prior gates. Launch checks verify earlier work; they do not replace it. Fed by P1 · Applicability / compliance discovery, P10 · Payments if applicable, P12 · Email / notifications.
