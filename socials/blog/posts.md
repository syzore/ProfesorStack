  ProfesorStack is not a product with a login. It is the skills I keep next to the code.

  /product-council is for before you commit to build. Advisors, not voters. A measured test: eleven seats found planted flaws and still lost to one agent with no roles, because the best finding lived between lanes. So you triage. Seat only the uncertainty. Always keep a generalist and someone whose job is to kill it. Run them in parallel so they cannot politely agree.

  /crap is a script. CRAP(m) = CC(m)² × (1 − Cov(m))³ + CC(m). Strictly above 30 is crappy. The script measures. You fix. Stdlib only.

  /compliance is a tree. If you do not store anything, do nothing. If you store what the user asked for, disclose. If you store analytics, consent. Most banners are interrupting people for a pixel they could have skipped, or asking in a way that does not count.

  Also: user-path-recognition (shorten the routes people actually take), simplify (names instead of comments), and so on.
